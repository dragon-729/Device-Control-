# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want DroidLink to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour
* Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent.
* Give and accept feedback graciously.
* Focus on what is best for users and the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour
* Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
* Publishing others' private information.
* **Using or promoting DroidLink for stalking, covert surveillance or unauthorised access to someone else's device.**
* Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this code. Report problems privately to the maintainer on
Telegram ([@YogeshBhai729](https://t.me/YogeshBhai729)) or through a GitHub private message. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
